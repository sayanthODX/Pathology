Inflammation at a Glance

This section provides a high-level quantitative analysis of the chemical mediators discussed in the report. By grouping mediators based on their primary physiological actions, you can visualize the redundancy and amplification inherent in the inflammatory response. Most mediators are short-lived and act on specific receptors to tightly regulate these processes.

Cell-Derived Mediators

Produced locally at the site of inflammation. They are either preformed in granules for rapid release or synthesized de novo.

  • Preformed: Histamine (Mast cells), Serotonin (Platelets)
  • Newly Synthesized: Prostaglandins, Leukotrienes, Lipoxins

Plasma-Protein-Derived

Synthesized mainly in the liver and circulate as inactive precursors. Require proteolytic cleavage for activation at the site.

  • Complement System: C3a, C5a, C3b, MAC
  • Kinins: Bradykinin
  • Coagulation: Factor XII activation products
